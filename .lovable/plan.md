# Remove "advisor / adviser" self-references site-wide

Balaji Nivesh must always be described as a mutual fund distributor. An audit of every page, e-mail, chatbot prompt and translation file found 60 places where the words "advisor" or "adviser" appear. They fall into three groups, and only the first group is a problem.

## 1. Wording to change (we call ourselves an advisor)

| Where the visitor sees it | Now | Change to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main menu item | "Find an Advisor" | "Find a Distributor" |
| Floating contact button (desktop) | "Talk to an advisor or ask a question" + WhatsApp message "I'd like to speak with an advisor." | "Talk to our team..." / "I'd like to speak with the Balaji Nivesh team." |
| Mobile bottom bar WhatsApp message | "talk to an advisor" | "talk to the Balaji Nivesh team" |
| Header WhatsApp message | "talk to an advisor" | "talk to the Balaji Nivesh team" |
| Calculator lead form | "15-min call with our SEBI-compliant advisor", "Our advisor will reach out..." (2 places) | "15-min call with our AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor", "Our team will reach out..." |
| Contact form success toast | "An advisor will WhatsApp you..." | "Our team will WhatsApp you..." |
| Next-step block | "Talk to a Balaji Nivesh advisor free for 15 minutes" | "Talk to the Balaji Nivesh team free for 15 minutes" |
| Time-on-page nudge | "Free 15-minute call with a qualified advisor" | "Free 15-minute call with our AMFI-registered distributor" |
| How-it-works step | "A qualified advisor walks you through the options." | "Our AMFI-registered distributor walks you through the options." |
| SIF solutions page | "We are a distributor, not an advisor" | "We are a distributor, not an investment adviser" (kept as a disclaimer sentence) |
| Admin partners screen (internal) | "sub-advisor" | "sub-distributor" |

Hindi and Bengali files carry no self-describing "advisor" phrases beyond the legal disclaimers, so no translation copy changes are needed; if any of the above keys are translated they will be updated to match.

## 2. Wording to keep — required legal disclaimers

Statements that we are **not** an adviser are mandated and stay exactly as they are: Disclaimer page, Terms of Use, Commission Disclosure, the SEBI disclaimer component, header compliance strip, solution-page meta descriptions, risk-profiler disclaimer, e-mail footer, and the three language files. These reference "SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013" and "not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser".

## 3. Wording to keep — pointing to other professionals

Suggestions to consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser, a licensed insurance advisor (life-cover calculator) or a tax advisor are correct and compliant, and remain.

## AI content generators

Chatbot, blog, academy, bible-chapter, daily and weekly market-update prompts already instruct "Distributor, never Adviser". One line will be added to each: never describe Balaji Nivesh or its staff as an advisor/adviser — use "mutual fund distributor". This keeps future auto-generated text clean.
