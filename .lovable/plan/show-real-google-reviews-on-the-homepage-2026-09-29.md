# Show real Google reviews on the homepage

## Goal
Build trust early in the visitor journey by showing real customer voices, not only the existing Google rating and a request to write a review.

## Homepage experience
- Move the current Google review area from near the bottom of the homepage to the early trust area, after the existing credibility strip. Keep its live rating and review count, “Read reviews” and “Write a review” links; do not duplicate that section elsewhere.
- Redesign it as a clean Balaji Nivesh review showcase: rating and Google attribution at the top, followed by a small selection of readable review excerpts with reviewer names, five-star visual treatment, and a discreet link to the Google listing. Use the site's orange/blue fintech styling without putting a card around the entire section.
- Show four of the supplied reviews whose excerpts focus on service, education, patience and trust: Ashish Manpuria, Ambi dey, Payeel Bhattacharya and Avighna Gupta. Keep each displayed excerpt word-for-word from its screenshot; clearly show an ellipsis if text is shortened. Do not use the screenshots themselves as page graphics, claim individual review links that were not supplied, or reproduce reviewer phrases calling Balaji Nivesh an adviser/financial planner. Avoid promises of returns or personalized advice in surrounding copy.
- Make the layout easy to scan on desktop and mobile. On small screens, show a clear swipeable sequence or compact stacked selection with no clipped text and accessible controls if scrolling is used. Keep the invitation to contact the team nearby, secondary to the review evidence.

## Accuracy and compliance
- Treat the screenshots as supplied source material, not as proof of a fresh live sync. Reviewer excerpts are a curated snapshot and must not be presented as automatically updating; only the existing rating/count retain their current daily-refresh behavior.
- Attribute the reviews to Google and retain the existing link to the listing so visitors can read the original reviews. Do not invent dates from relative screenshot timestamps (“9 weeks ago”, “3 days ago”). If rating data is unavailable, the review excerpts should remain visible without a fabricated overall score.

## Technical details and checks
- Update the existing homepage reviews component and its placement rather than adding another testimonial system or data table. Reuse current site settings and Google link helpers.
- Check the excerpts against the supplied images and compliance terminology; test the homepage, Google links, and desktop/mobile layouts. Confirm no duplicate review area, console errors or preview build errors.
